Q+A Katha - Thirukkural 🧠

Question: Who directed the 1973 film "American Graffiti"?

Answer: George Lucas - Entertainment: Film. Itha kelvi Thirukkural pathi - Answer George Lucas. Thirukkural pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Thirukkural pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Thirukkural oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Thirukkural ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Thirukkural la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.
Q+A Katha - Pazhamozhi 🧠

Question: Which movie sequel had improved box office results compared to its original film?

Answer: Toy Story 2 - Entertainment: Film. Itha kelvi Pazhamozhi pathi - Answer Toy Story 2. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Pazhamozhi pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Pazhamozhi la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Pazhamozhi oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
Q+A Katha - Joke 🧠

Question: Which Native American tribe/nation requires at least one half blood quantum (equivalent to one parent) to be eligible for membership?

Answer: Yomba Shoshone Tribe - Politics. Itha kelvi Joke pathi - Answer Yomba Shoshone Tribe. Oru kaalathula oru oor la Joke nu oru periya vishayam irunthuchu, yaarum atha purinjikama irunthanga, oru chinna paiyan thaan Joke oda arumaiya purinjikittan, avan Joke pathi ellaar kitta yum pesi puriya vachan, mella mella oor makkal ellam Joke ah purinjikittanga, antha oor ye Joke nala azhaga maariduchu, ithula irunthu namakku theriyura vishayam enna na, Joke ah purinjikita life ye azhagahum.
