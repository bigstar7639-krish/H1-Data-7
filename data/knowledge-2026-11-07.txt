## 1️⃣ Physics‑la (Mechanics)

| Concept | Formula | Meaning |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Power (P)** | `P = W / t` | Work (`W` – joules) split by time (`t` – seconds). |
| **Alternative** | `P = F · v` | Force (`F` – newtons) * velocity (`v` – m/s). |
| **Unit** | **Watt (W)** | 1 W = 1 J/s. |

**Example**: Oru 10 kg weight‑a 5 m height‑kku lift pannalam. Work = m·g·h = 10·9.8·5 ≈ 490 J. If it takes 2 seconds, power = 490 J / 2 s = **245 W**.
