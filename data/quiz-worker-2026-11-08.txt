Q+A Katha - Teacher 🧠

Question: In board games, an additional or ammended rule that applies to a certain group or place is informally known as a "what" rule?

Answer: House - Entertainment: Board Games. Itha kelvi Teacher pathi - Answer House. Teacher pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Teacher pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Teacher oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Teacher ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Teacher la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.
Q+A Katha - Student 🧠

Question: Gwyneth Paltrow has a daughter named...?

Answer: Apple - Celebrities. Itha kelvi Student pathi - Answer Apple. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Student pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Student la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Student oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
