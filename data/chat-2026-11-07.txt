## 📍 Where is it?
- **Location:** Southern tip of India, bordered by **Andaman Sea** (east), **Karnataka** (north‑west), **Kerala** (west) & **Lakshadweep** (south‑west).  
- **Capital:** **Chennai** – a bustling metro, IT hub & cultural hotspot.

## 🏛️ Quick History Bits
- One of the world’s **oldest continuous civilizations** – the Sangam era (3rd c. BC – 3rd c. AD) produced epic poems in **Old Tamil**.  
- **Chola Empire** (9th‑13th c.) ruled over South‑East Asia, building the majestic **Brihadeeswarar Temple** in Thanjavur.  
- Under British rule it became **Madras Presidency**, later renamed Tamil Nadu in 1969.
