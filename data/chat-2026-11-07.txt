## 1️⃣ Basics – Moon எது?  
- **Scientific name:** Luna (லூனா) / Moon (மூன்)  
- **Type:** Natural satellite of Earth.  
- **Distance from Earth:** ~384,400 km (≈238,855 miles).  
- **Diameter:** 3,474 km (≈2,159 miles) – about 1/4 of Earth's diameter.  
- **Mass:** 7.35 × 10²² kg (0.012 % of Earth’s mass).  
- **Gravity:** 0.165 g (≈1/6 of Earth's).
